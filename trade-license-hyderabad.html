Trade License FAQs

What is a Trade License?

A Trade License is a local regulatory permission that may apply to certain business activities. The applicable requirements depend on the relevant local authority and business circumstances.

Can you help with Trade License registration?

Yes. Shiva Associates provides assistance with the applicable application process and document preparation.

What documents are required?

Requirements can vary based on the business activity, premises and applicable local rules. Contact us for a checklist relevant to your business.

Does every business need a Trade License?

Not necessarily. Whether a license is applicable depends on the business activity and the rules of the relevant local authority.
