Common Questions

ESI & PF registration FAQs

What are ESI and PF?

ESI and EPF are statutory social-security frameworks for eligible employees and establishments. Applicability depends on the relevant law and circumstances.

Can you help with ESI and PF registration?

Yes. Shiva Associates provides assistance with applicable employer registration processes and document preparation.

Is ESI or PF registration mandatory for every business?

No. Applicability depends on factors such as the establishment, employee strength and other prescribed conditions.

What information is required?

Requirements can vary according to the establishment and applicable registration. Contact us for a checklist relevant to your business.
