Common Questions

IEC registration FAQs

What is IEC?

IEC stands for Import Export Code. It is used for applicable import and export activities under the relevant regulations.

Can you help with IEC registration?

Yes. Shiva Associates provides assistance with the applicable IEC registration process and document preparation.

What documents are required?

Requirements can vary according to the business and application circumstances. Contact us for a checklist relevant to your business.

Is IEC required for every business?

Not every business needs IEC. Applicability depends on the nature of the transaction and the applicable rules, including any prescribed exceptions.
