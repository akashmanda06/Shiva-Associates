About Us

A reliable partner for tax filings and statutory compliance

Shiva Associates is a taxation and business compliance consultancy based in Yousufguda, Hyderabad. We work with salaried individuals, traders, shop owners, startups and established firms, helping them stay correct and up to date with every statutory requirement.

Our work covers the full compliance cycle — registering your business, obtaining the right licenses, filing GST and income tax returns, and completing labour and employee-related registrations such as ESI and PF. We also assist with PAN, TAN, digital signatures, MSME and IEC code registration, PF withdrawal and passport documentation.

Every engagement is handled personally. You get a clear list of the documents needed, a straightforward explanation of the process, and regular updates until the work is complete.
